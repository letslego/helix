title: Material non-public information (MNPI)
tags: [policy, compliance, mnpi]
---

Treat the following as **MNPI** when it is not already public:

- Unpublished earnings, guidance, M&A, or financing plans
- Unannounced product, customer, or vendor deals
- Internal headcount, pricing, or capacity plans tied to a listed issuer
- Any fact a reasonable investor would consider material that is not public

Rules for specialists:

- Do **not** recommend trades, tips, or “buy/sell” language about issuers.
- Do **not** paste MNPI into external channels, tickets, or unapproved tools.
- Prefer high-level, non-attributable summaries when MNPI might be present.
- If unsure whether something is MNPI, withhold detail and flag for compliance.
- Never mix MNPI from one tenant into another tenant’s context.
